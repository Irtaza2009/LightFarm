# Light Farm

An **idle** **clicker** game where you farm light from fireflies!!

Made for **overglade**! 

**Play at:** https://irtaza.itch.io/light-farm

## Controls

- Click and drag the fireflies to the purple core to collect light!
- Sell the light to get coins!
- Buy the orange pillar to keep the fireflies in one place (hint: place the orange pillar near the purple core)
- Click and drag to pan the camera
- Buy more land and fireflies and expand your light farm!
